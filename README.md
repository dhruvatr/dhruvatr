### 01 / HONEY CHAIN

**IoT Beehive Monitoring & Honey Traceability**

`INTERNET OF THINGS` · `EMBEDDED SYSTEMS` · `TRACEABILITY`

An IoT-based beehive monitoring and traceability system designed to monitor hive conditions and track honey throughout the supply chain.

**Engineering components**

- ESP32-based monitoring architecture.
- Temperature and humidity sensing.
- HX711 load-cell integration.
- Acoustic monitoring of bee activity.
- Bee audio frequency analysis.
- QR-based traceability across the honey supply chain.

**Traceability workflow**

`HIVE → HARVEST → PROCESSING → PACKAGING → DISTRIBUTION → CONSUMER`
